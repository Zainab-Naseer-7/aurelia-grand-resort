The Aurelia Philosophy

A Sanctuary Beyond Ordinary

“Architecture sculpted by sea breezes, silence guarded by emerald cliffs, and moments refined by pure intuitive hospitality.”

Perched exclusively along the sun-drenched coastline of Cala di Oro, Aurelia Grand Resort was conceived as an architectural tribute to the elements. Here, dramatic limestone cliffs cradle private lagoons, while our biophilic villas seamlessly blend indoor luxury with untamed Mediterranean beauty.

Whether arriving via private helicopter at our cliffside helipad or docking your yacht at the marina pier, every step into Aurelia reveals a rare ecosystem of Michelin-caliber gastronomy, restorative thermal wellness, and thoughtful serenity.

Heritage & Biophilic Architecture

Crafted with Reverence for Nature

Founded in 2001 by architectural visionary Matteo Vane and luxury hotelier Elena Rossi, Aurelia Grand Resort was envisioned not to conquer the cliffside, but to complete it. Every stone wall was hand-carved from native limestone excavated during construction.

Today, Aurelia operates on 100% renewable geothermal and solar energy, supports an adjacent 50-hectare coral marine preserve, and harvests fresh heritage produce from our rooftop biodynamic orchards.

Carbon-Positive Seclusion

Certified green luxury with advanced water desalination and zero single-use plastics.

Marine Preservation

10% of all private charter proceeds are invested into Mediterranean coral restoration.
